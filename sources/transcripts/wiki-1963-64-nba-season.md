The San Francisco Warriors, in their second season in California, won the West with 48 wins in 80 NBA games.

The Warriors had five other ten-point scorers and a potential future star in rookie Nate Thurmond under coach Alex Hannum.

San Francisco dismissed the Hawks four games to three.
