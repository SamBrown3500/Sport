The loss against the Rockets was particularly painful for Robinson because it occurred in the Western Conference Finals with Robinson playing head-to-head against his chief rival, Hakeem Olajuwon.

By his own admission, Robinson was outplayed by Olajuwon in the series, their only meetings in post-season play.
