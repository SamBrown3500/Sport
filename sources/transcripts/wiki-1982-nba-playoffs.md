Held on Mother's Day, Game 1 was infamously known as the "Mother's Day Massacre" as Boston beat Philadelphia by 40 points, which remains the 76ers' worst playoff loss to date.

The Celtics were not able to overcome a 3–1 series deficit like they had in last season's Eastern Conference finals, losing Game 7 at home.

The Boston Garden crowd in Game 7 chanted "Beat LA! Beat LA!" to the victorious Sixers.
