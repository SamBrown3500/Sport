The Western Conference champion Los Angeles Lakers defeated the defending NBA champion and Eastern Conference champion Boston Celtics in six games to win their tenth NBA championship, becoming the second team in league history to win ten or more championships. The key moment of the series was Magic Johnson's Junior sky hook in Game 4.
Much was at stake in the 1987 NBA Finals. Rivals since their days in college, Larry Bird and Magic Johnson entered the Finals each with three NBA championships. In terms of the Celtics–Lakers, each team also had won three championships in the 1980s. With the win in 1987 and the following year, the Lakers and Johnson would surpass their rival and be seen as the team and player of the 1980s.
