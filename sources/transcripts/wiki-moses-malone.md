During the 1981-82 NBA season, Malone averaged 31.1 points with 14.7 rebounds and won his second MVP award.
Malone was named the league MVP for the second straight year, becoming the only NBA player ever to win the award in consecutive seasons with two different teams, a feat matched only by Barry Bonds (1992–1993) and Shohei Ohtani (2023–2024) in the four major American sports leagues.
